These notes describe the purpose and scope of selected work. They are not independent product certifications or audit reports.

## OceanCentinell Enterprise

**Area:** maritime cybersecurity and operational visibility.

The project explores how operational and security information can be organized for monitoring and evaluation. Its presentation includes architecture documentation, workflows and technical evaluation.

**Work represented:** system design, information organization, documentation and platform development.

**Scope:** external integrations, real-data operation and production implementation need specific verification. A service health response alone does not establish that all capabilities work.

## Centinell Forensics / ForensicCentinell

**Area:** digital investigation and case documentation.

The project is intended to organize investigations and relate cases, records and evidence documentation.

**Work represented:** investigation workflows, technical documentation and development of tools that support analyst decisions.

**Scope:** available features depend on the evaluated version. Suitability for handling real evidence must be assessed before that use.

## SkyguardCentinell Twins

**Area:** applied research and reproducible experiments.

The documented civilian baseline uses synthetic temperatures in offline sensor-fusion experiments and continuity between two simulated nodes. It includes reports and consistency checks.

**Work represented:** experiment design, models, comparison of results and traceability.

**Scope:** simulation results apply to the defined scenarios. They do not establish physical-sensor performance, aviation certification or mission capability. This remains a research project.

## My First Words Kids

**Area:** educational applications.

[Public repository](https://github.com/Centinell2025/My-First-Words-20-Games-Sound-v5.zip)

The web/PWA project provides parent-guided early-vocabulary activities using pictures and sound. Its repository documents twenty topics, eight languages and learning-language pairs.

**Work represented:** activity design, interface, content and educational application development.

**Scope:** speech availability depends on the browser and operating system. Translations require native-speaker editorial review. Web-source publication does not establish Google Play or App Store distribution.

## Victoria en Cristo Jesús LLC

**Area:** Christian music production and creative direction.

The visual portfolio includes two short excerpts from existing recordings: **Jesus, My Messiah** and **Salt of the Earth**. Each sample selects source time 00:30–00:55, with short fades at the boundaries for presentation. The recordings are presented as creative work using an AI-assisted production workflow.

The waveform illustrations are computed from the actual sample audio. Embedded players require a user action to start playback. Full recordings and unrelated source materials are not included in the public portfolio.
